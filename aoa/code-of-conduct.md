# Code of Conduct: Use of Name, Logo, and Marks

**Adopted under Article 11 of the Articles of Association**
**British Violin Making Association Limited**

---

## 1. Purpose

This Code of Conduct is made by the Management Committee under Article 11 of the Articles of Association. It governs the use of the Company's name, logo, and marks by Members.

## 2. General Principle

The title and logo of the British Violin Making Association must not be used for promotional material or to imply that a particular standard of quality has been achieved.

## 3. Restrictions on Use

BVMA membership does not guarantee any professional standard. Therefore, neither the logo nor the mentioning of one's membership should appear in any Member's promotional material, including but not limited to:

- Printed adverts, leaflets, and flyers
- Letterheads and compliment slips
- Business cards
- Social media profiles and posts
- Websites

## 4. Directory Listings

Listings in directories may, for informative purposes, contain the fact that one is a Member, but only in the actual listing and not in any accompanying advertisement.

## 5. Amendment

This Code of Conduct may be amended by resolution of the Management Committee at any time.
